Live notes

Speech is written down as people talk; the notes then become draft minutes. It hears this device's microphone: in an online call, use the speaker (not earphones) so other people's voices are picked up too.

This browser cannot listen. Use Chrome, Edge or Safari for live notes, or type the notes below.
