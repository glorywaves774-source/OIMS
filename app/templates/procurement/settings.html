The estimated value of a purchase request decides how it must be bought (amounts in {{ org.currency }}):

  • Below the first amount: one quotation.
  • From the first amount: at least three quotations.
  • From the second amount: a competitive tender — at least three bids and the evaluation committee's report.

If the chosen quotation is not the lowest, a reason must be written down.
